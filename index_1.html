Car warranty live chat: test page

Use the chat button in the bottom-right corner to try each journey below. The bot asks a few questions, then hands you to the right team, just as a real customer would experience it.

Chat button: bottom right of this page

How to test

Each test takes a minute or two. Please work through as many as you can.

1
Pick a scenarioChoose one from the list below and note its number.
2
Open the chatFill in the name and email form (test details are fine), then follow the path shown.
3
Check where you landConfirm the right team picked the chat up, or you saw the expected message.
4
Tick it offTick the box, and note anything odd: wording, delays, wrong team.
